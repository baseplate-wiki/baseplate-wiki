InCommand is an alternative to the Roblox Command Bar that allows you to save scripts and execute them at the plugin execution level. InCommand was released in 2020 when the Roblox Command Bar was lackluster. Since then, many updates and improvements have happened to the command bar, making InCommand less and less beneficial over the vanilla Roblox experience. Despite previously being paid, InCommand is now available for free on the Roblox Creator Store.
